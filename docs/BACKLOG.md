## Stitch-count estimator (later)
Customers never know stitch count. Build an estimate from their art:
- Phil supplies 10–20 pairs: finished .DST sewout (header has stitch count + size) + the original customer art + placement.
- Measure stitched coverage of the art at sew size (text / fill / outline), fit HCD's own stitches-per-sq-inch.
- Output only needs the right price bucket (apparel: ≤10k, 10–12k, 12–15k, 15–21k; hats: ≤8k, 8–10k, 10–21k); flag designs near a cutoff.
- Every newly digitized job feeds its real count back in to refine the estimate.
Until then, email intake assumes up to 10,000 stitches on apparel and up to 8,000 on hats (confirm after digitizing).

## DTF cost deep dive
DTF is priced at replacement cost (Supacolor) for now. Work out what a transfer really costs in-house:
- Hydra 9-color 6-head: $1,000/mo lease, $300–700/mo supplies, $20/hr labor, 180–200 sq ft/hr.
- Volume: about two 22" x 640' rolls a month (~2,350 sq ft).
- Still needed: actual supply spend for the last 2–3 months, film usage per gang sheet, and pressing time per piece.
- Goal: true cost per 4x4 / 11x5 / 11x14 and margin at each price break.

## Wholesale DTF (maybe, later)
The Hydra runs about 12 hrs a month out of 150+ possible. Selling transfers could cover the lease.
Phil's view: only self-serve with firm guardrails, never a handholding service. No panic-driven shops.
- Online ordering, prepaid, customer uploads print-ready gang sheets.
- "Your art, your result": no art fixes, no reprints for bad files.
- Fixed turnaround, no rush (or a steep rush fee); pickup locker or shipping.
- If tested, start with a few trusted shops before building anything public.
